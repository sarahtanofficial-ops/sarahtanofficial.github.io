Profile

I'm a curious problem solver who loves figuring out what people actually need, and designing something that makes their experience simpler. Now I'm channeling that same curiosity into Computer Science (anticipated graduation 2027) and human-centered experience design, building skills in Figma, web and app design, programming, and data analysis along the way. Multilingual (English, Korean, Japanese) and rooted in a background in Asian and Middle Eastern Studies, I bring a genuine passion for inclusive design and simplifying complex experiences to every project I take on.

Selected Work

Campus Transportation & Parking Redesign

Team Project · DES 3131, User Experience Design

Problem

There are many challenges students face with parking on campus, particularly the lack of flexibility in contracts and the absence of user-friendly software to manage them. Parking spaces on campus, especially parking long term, can be a complex and stressful matter.

Process

Working with a team of student designers, I helped redesign the site's information architecture and prototype a new parking strategy in Figma, focused on surfacing the information students actually needed faster.

Outcome

A high-fidelity interactive prototype for a redesigned campus transportation website with a clearer, more student-friendly parking strategy. Explore the working prototype below.
